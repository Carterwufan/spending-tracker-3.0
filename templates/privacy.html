What we store

  • Account details: your email address and a one-way scrypt hash of your password. Your actual password is never stored.
  • Transactions: the dates, types, categories, amounts and notes you enter.
  • Security records: a count of recent failed sign-in attempts for an email address (kept up to one day), and, if you reset your password, the time of the request and a hashed one-time token that expires after 30 minutes.

We use this information only to run the app for you. We do not sell it, show ads, or use analytics or tracking services.
